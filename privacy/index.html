Privacy policy

AI Scanner: PDF Maker & OCR (com.krmndigital.aiscanner) · KRMN Digital · effective 10 November 2026

Summary: AI Scanner has no account system and no server. Your scans, the text it recognizes, summaries and translations are made on your phone and stay there unless you share them. The free version shows ads through Google AdMob, which processes some data as described below; AI Scanner Pro removes the ads.

What the app stores on your phone

  • The photos you scan, the finished pages, the text recognized from them, your signatures and the PDF files you import or create.
  • Your settings (language, theme, scan options) and whether Pro is active.

These files live in the app's private storage, are excluded from Android backups, are never uploaded by the app, and are deleted when you uninstall it. Text recognition (OCR), summaries, signatures and every PDF tool run entirely on the device.
